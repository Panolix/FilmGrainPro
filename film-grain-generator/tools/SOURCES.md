# Grain data sources

Collected to characterise the 35 film stocks. Primary sources are manufacturer data sheets; anything not published by the manufacturer is marked `estimated`/`derived`.

## kodak_f4017
- **KODAK PROFESSIONAL TRI-X 400 Film - Technical Data F-4017 (2007)**
- https://125px.com/docs/film/kodak/f4017-400TX-2007.pdf
- Provides: Diffuse RMS granularity (48 um aperture, net density 1.0, 12x, HC-110 B)

## kodak_f4016
- **KODAK PROFESSIONAL T-MAX 100 Film - Technical Data F-4016 (2018)**
- https://125px.com/docs/film/kodak/f4016_tmax_100-2018.pdf
- Provides: Diffuse RMS granularity + resolving power (D-76)

## iso10505
- **ISO 10505:2009 - Photography: Root mean square granularity of photographic films**
- https://www.iso.org/standard/41634.html
- Provides: Standard measurement definition for RMS granularity (48 um aperture, D=1.0)

## wikipedia_filmgrain
- **Wikipedia - Film grain (RMS granularity, Selwyn granularity)**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_grain
- Provides: Physical definitions and Selwyn's law

## wikipedia_tabular
- **Wikipedia - Tabular-grain film**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tabular-grain_film
- Provides: Emulsion morphology: tabular vs cubic, T-GRAIN, core-shell

## wikipedia_halation
- **Wikipedia - Anti-halation backing / halation**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-halation_backing
- Provides: Halation mechanism (red blow-out around highlights when anti-halation/rem-jet is absent)

## kodak_tgrain_paper
- **Kofron & Booms, 'KODAK T-Grain Emulsions in Color Films', J. Soc. Photogr. Sci. Tech. Japan 49(6), 1986**
- https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/photogrst1964/49/6/49_6_499/_pdf
- Provides: Tabular grain dimensions: equivalent circular diameter 2.33 um, thickness 0.12 um (aspect 22.6:1) for an experimental emulsion; 3:1-5:1 aspect for production tabular colour emulsions; cubic reference emulsions ~1-1.4 um. Class-level size ranges only.
