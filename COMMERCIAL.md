# Commercial licensing

FilmGrainPro is free for non-commercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
This page covers the separate license you need in order to use it
commercially.

## Do I need a commercial license?

**Yes**, if you want to:

- sell FilmGrainPro or a modified version of it;
- bundle it into a paid product, installer, subscription or service;
- use it to deliver paid work as part of what you offer customers;
- ship it inside a company product or as part of a commercial workflow.

**No**, if your use is personal, educational, academic, or part of a
non-commercial open-source project.

## What a commercial license gives you

- the right to use, modify and distribute the software commercially;
- relief from the NonCommercial condition of CC BY-NC-SA 4.0;
- the option to negotiate away ShareAlike, so your changes can stay private;
- a signed agreement your legal team can rely on.

Scope, term and price are agreed case by case. A flat fee, a revenue share, or
a mix of both are all possible.

## What it does not give you

- rights to the "FilmGrainPro" name, logo, or "City Frame Photography"
  branding (separate agreement required);
- any warranty beyond what the agreement states.

## How to request one

Email **info@cityframe-photography.com** with:

1. who you are and what you want to build;
2. which project(s) you need;
3. whether you will distribute modified versions.

If you want to contribute code, see [CLA.md](CLA.md). Contributions stay
non-commercial for everyone else, but the CLA keeps commercial licensing
